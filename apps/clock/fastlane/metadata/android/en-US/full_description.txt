Alarms, timers, a stopwatch and a world clock in one app.

* Alarms that repeat on chosen days, with gentle (rising) volume, snooze and vibration
* Several timers at once, with labels you type in place
* A stopwatch with laps, also as a Quick Settings tile
* World clock with cities and their time difference
* Home-screen clock widget and launcher shortcuts for Timer and Stopwatch


Part of Tessera, a suite of free apps that replace Google's defaults with Material 3 Expressive design, dynamic colour and dark mode. No ads, no tracking, no Google services. Licensed under GPL-3.0.
