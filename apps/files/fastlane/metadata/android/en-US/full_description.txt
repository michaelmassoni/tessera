A file manager for your phone's storage and SD cards.

* Browse, search, sort and select; copy, move, rename and share
* Copies and moves keep going in the background, with progress and Cancel
* Compress to Zip or TAR.GZ and open archives to see what's inside before extracting
* Bookmarks for the folders you use most, and a trash you can restore from
* A built-in PDF viewer

Files asks for all-files access, as a file manager needs it to show and manage everything on your storage.


Part of Tessera, a suite of free apps that replace Google's defaults with Material 3 Expressive design, dynamic colour and dark mode. No ads, no tracking, no Google services. Licensed under GPL-3.0.
