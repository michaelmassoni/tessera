Follow your shows and listen your way.

* Search for shows, or add one by its feed address
* New episodes on Home, with notifications, and an Up Next queue
* Downloads for listening offline
* Playback speed, a sleep timer and casting
* Android Auto

Show search uses the Apple Podcasts and fyyd directories; only your search words are sent.


Part of Tessera, a suite of free apps that replace Google's defaults with Material 3 Expressive design, dynamic colour and dark mode. No ads, no tracking, no Google services. Licensed under GPL-3.0.
