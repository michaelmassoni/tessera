A calendar for your own events, synced with your own server.

* Month, week, day and schedule views
* Repeating events, reminders and search
* Month and agenda widgets you can set up
* Import ICS files
* Sync with your own CalDAV server (Nextcloud, Radicale and others), no Google account needed


Part of Tessera, a suite of free apps that replace Google's defaults with Material 3 Expressive design, dynamic colour and dark mode. No ads, no tracking, no Google services. Licensed under GPL-3.0.
