Quick notes and checklists, organised your way.

* Text notes and checklists, with links you can tap
* Labels, colours, pins, archive and trash
* Reminders at a time you choose
* Search across all your notes
* Home-screen widget, a launcher shortcut and a Quick Settings tile for a new note
* Back up and restore your notes


Part of Tessera, a suite of free apps that replace Google's defaults with Material 3 Expressive design, dynamic colour and dark mode. No ads, no tracking, no Google services. Licensed under GPL-3.0.
