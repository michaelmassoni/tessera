A phone app for calls, recents and contacts.

* Favourites, recents (with search and a Missed filter), contacts and a keypad
* The in-call screen: hold, mute, speaker, Bluetooth, keypad and call waiting
* Decline with a quick text reply
* Caller location for unknown numbers and spam reports, worked out on your phone
* Copy and paste numbers on the keypad; dual-SIM choice when you call

Phone needs to be your default phone app to handle calls.


Part of Tessera, a suite of free apps that replace Google's defaults with Material 3 Expressive design, dynamic colour and dark mode. No ads, no tracking, no Google services. Licensed under GPL-3.0.
