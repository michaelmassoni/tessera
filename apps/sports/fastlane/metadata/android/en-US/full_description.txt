Follow your teams across leagues.

* Scores, fixtures and standings, and a page for each team
* A live score on the lock screen while your team plays
* A spoiler-free mode that hides scores until you want them
* A home-screen widget

Scores come from ESPN's public data service.


Part of Tessera, a suite of free apps that replace Google's defaults with Material 3 Expressive design, dynamic colour and dark mode. No ads, no tracking, no Google services. Licensed under GPL-3.0.
