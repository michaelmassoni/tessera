Contacts on your phone, kept in sync with your own server.

* Search, favourites and a full editor with photos
* Finds and merges duplicate contacts
* Import and export vCard files
* Sync with your own CardDAV server (Nextcloud, Radicale and others), no Google account needed
* Shows each number and email once, even when a contact is saved in several places


Part of Tessera, a suite of free apps that replace Google's defaults with Material 3 Expressive design, dynamic colour and dark mode. No ads, no tracking, no Google services. Licensed under GPL-3.0.
