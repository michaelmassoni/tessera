A voice recorder that keeps going when you leave the app.

* A live waveform while you record
* Records in the background, with a Quick Settings tile to start and stop
* Search, rename and share your recordings
* Play back at any speed


Part of Tessera, a suite of free apps that replace Google's defaults with Material 3 Expressive design, dynamic colour and dark mode. No ads, no tracking, no Google services. Licensed under GPL-3.0.
