The weather for where you are and the places you save.

* Hourly and daily forecasts, with rain, wind, UV and more
* Weather warnings and air quality
* Forecast and date-and-weather widgets you can set up
* Your location (approximate) or any city you search for

Forecasts come from Open-Meteo, and in Australia from the Bureau of Meteorology. Only the place's coordinates are sent.


Part of Tessera, a suite of free apps that replace Google's defaults with Material 3 Expressive design, dynamic colour and dark mode. No ads, no tracking, no Google services. Licensed under GPL-3.0.
