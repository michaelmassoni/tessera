A text messaging app for SMS and MMS.

* Conversations with pictures, groups and contact photos
* Search inside your messages
* Tap links, emails and numbers; long-press to copy them
* Scheduled send, block, archive and pin
* Android Auto

Messages needs to be your default SMS app to send and receive.


Part of Tessera, a suite of free apps that replace Google's defaults with Material 3 Expressive design, dynamic colour and dark mode. No ads, no tracking, no Google services. Licensed under GPL-3.0.
