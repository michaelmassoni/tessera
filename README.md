Free, open-source replacements for the apps that come with your phone. Thirteen apps that look and feel
first-party on current Android, with no ads, no tracking and no Google Play Services.

> [!NOTE]
> Tessera is young. The apps are in everyday use but still on 0.x versions, and Phone is
> experimental. Each app is installed and updated on its own, so try the ones you want.

## Why Tessera?

A *tessera* is one tile in a mosaic. Each app is its own tile, and together they share one look.

- **Feels built in.** Material 3 Expressive, colours from your wallpaper, dark mode, edge-to-edge,
  predictive back, and layouts for phones, foldables and tablets.
- **Private by design.** No ads, trackers, analytics or crash reporters, and no internet access
  unless an app needs it. Weather (Bureau of Meteorology in Australia, Open-Meteo elsewhere) and
  Sports (scores from ESPN) fetch public data with no account or key. Music and Tasks talk only to a
  server you add yourself (Subsonic or CalDAV), and Music looks up lyrics only if you turn that on.
  Nothing about you is sent anywhere else.
- **Works on de-Googled phones.** Nothing depends on Google Play Services, so the apps work the same
  on GrapheneOS, LineageOS or a stock phone.
- **One theme for all of them.** Choose light, dark or system, wallpaper colours and pure black once,
  and every Tessera app follows. Each app also offers Colour, Light, Dark or custom-coloured icons.

## Install

**[Obtainium](https://obtainium.imranr.dev/)** (recommended) installs the apps straight from this
repository's releases and keeps them up to date.

- **One app:** tap its Obtainium badge in [the table above](#the-apps) on your phone.
- **All of them:** download [obtainium.json](docs/obtainium.json), then in Obtainium open
  **Import/Export → Obtainium import** and choose the file.

All the apps live in this one repository, so each Obtainium entry filters releases by the app's
name. If you add the repository by hand, set **Filter release titles by regular expression** to
`^Tessera Notes ` (for example) and **Filter APKs by regular expression** to `^tessera-notes-`.

You can also download APKs from [Releases][releases] and install them yourself.

Releases are signed with this certificate (SHA-256), if you want to check:
`25:CC:BB:FF:35:E9:14:7B:00:DE:D2:86:0A:E9:79:4F:E4:03:21:6E:8F:27:8E:93:AD:68:0D:C0:0E:E7:8F:85`

## Good to know

- **Android 12 or newer** is needed.
- **Phone and Messages** become fully useful once you make them your default phone and SMS apps
  (each app asks). Phone is experimental: it's safe to try, and you can switch back any time.
- **Files** asks for "All files access", so it can browse and manage everything on your phone and
  SD card, like the built-in file manager. Nothing leaves your device.
- **Tasks** asks to set exact alarms, so reminders (medications, say) arrive on the minute. It
  works without, but Android may then deliver a reminder up to an hour late.
- **Music and Tasks** can use your own server: Music any Subsonic-compatible server (Navidrome and
  others), Tasks a CalDAV server with task lists (tested with Radicale, which OpenCloud uses). Plain `http://` is
  only allowed on your home network or Tailscale.
- **Lots of GitHub checks in Obtainium?** GitHub limits checks without a token. Add a fine-grained
  personal access token with no permissions (public repositories, read-only) in Obtainium's
  settings under GitHub.

## Building

You need JDK 17 or newer (21 recommended) and the Android SDK with platform `android-37.0` and
build-tools `37.0.0`.

```sh
./gradlew assembleDebug check
```

APKs land in `apps/<app>/build/outputs/apk/debug/`. `check` runs Android Lint, ktlint, unit tests
and screenshot tests in light and dark. On Nix, `nix develop` gives you the whole toolchain, and
`nix develop .#emulator` adds an Android 17 emulator.

How the project is organised, and the conventions every app follows, are in
[CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md). Decisions and their reasons are in [docs/decisions.md](docs/decisions.md),
and what's planned next is in [docs/feature-gaps.md](docs/feature-gaps.md).
